How this practice app works

Questions come from real Division B invitational tests. Questions that share a story, table or chart are shown together as one set.

Reveal shows the answer, a short explanation, and — when your reference sheet (Sangchul Bae's Disease Detective Note) does not already have what you need — a cheat-sheet suggestion you can copy onto your sheet.

The order is shuffled. Your place is saved on this device only, so you pick up where you left off. After you finish every set, the deck is reshuffled into a new order.

Some answer keys had errors or unreadable answers. Those are marked with a purple note so you know the answer was checked or worked out by hand.
